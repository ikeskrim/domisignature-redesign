Your Wedding Journey with Domisignature

by domisignature - we know how to plan

  • Step 1 — Choose and Book Your Villa & Wedding Date

    Select your preferred Domisi villa and secure your wedding date. Each villa offers privacy, natural surroundings, and flexible spaces for ceremonies and celebrations.

  • Step 2 — Share Your Vision

    Tell us about your style, atmosphere, and priorities. Elegant, relaxed, rustic, or modern — we listen and build around your story.

  • Step 3 — Define Guests & Budget

    Together we clarify: – guest count – overall budget – key priorities. This helps us create a realistic plan from the very beginning.

  • Step 4 — Mood Board & Concept

    We create a visual mood board based on your preferences — colors, textures, décor, lighting, and overall feeling, so your wedding has a clear identity before moving forward.

  • Step 5 — Planning & Coordination

    We take care of the all organization so you can enjoy the process - from layout and décor to trusted local partners, timelines, and logistics.

  • Step 6 — Come and get married!

Venue

Set on a private 65-acre estate, Mountain Escape is a luxury wedding villa offering total privacy, three pools, panoramic mountain and sea views, and space to celebrate.

Thalasses

Venue

Thallases Villas includes four independent luxury villas located directly on a private beach, offering high-quality facilities and hotel-level comfort with far more privacy.

Each villa can be rented separately, or the entire complex can be reserved for larger wedding groups.


When booked as a full estate, guests enjoy: 

– 4 independent villas
– 9 bedrooms
– 6 bathrooms
– accommodation for up to 18 guests
– private beach just 50 meters away

All villas are designed to offer sea views and personal privacy, allowing everyone to stay together while still having their own space:

 The front villas (Thoi & Persi) are single-storey.

The rear villas (Eeanthe & Melia) are two-storey.

Ideal for seaside weddings, relaxed welcome dinners, and multi-day celebrations.

 A major advantage for weddings:

●       Sea view location for ceremonies and parties

●       LGBTQ+ friendly

●       Cycladic white and blue style - feel yourself in Greece!

●       Calm surroundings and direct beach access

 How many people can fit: up to 300

Olive Stories

Venue

Olive Stories is our open countryside wedding space. A large natural field with electricity and water, created for couples who love rustic parties and authentic outdoor vibes.

It has all the necessary amenities to provide you with a magical night under the stars.

This is where long tables, fairy lights on the olive trees, live music, and barefoot dancing feel at home.

Because it’s open land, celebrations aren’t limited by city noise restrictions — meaning your party doesn’t need to stop early.

 Perfect for:

●       Rustic weddings

●       Boho celebrations

●       Relaxed receptions

●       Authentic Cretan-style gatherings

●       LGBTQ+ friendly

●       Raw, simple, and full of character. 

How many people can fit: up to 200-300

  • Category: Venue

Villa Aetos

Venue

Villa Aetos is hidden amongst greenery and hills of Rethymno in Crete. It is designed for small, meaningful weddings where every guest matters and every moment feels personal.

This venue offers a rustic, cozy atmosphere inspired by the Greek countryside. It’s a quiet setting where ceremonies feel natural, dinners feel relaxed, and celebrations flow without pressure.

The villa accommodates up to 12 overnight guests across 6 bedrooms, spread over two levels and 180 m², which is ideal for couples who want to stay together with their closest people.

Because of its secluded location, Villa Aetos allows you to enjoy serenity and peace away from the city's noise.

Perfect for:

●       Intimate wedding ceremonies

●       Relaxed receptions

●       Multi-day celebrations with family or friends

●       LGBTQ+ friendly

Please note that parties are allowed until 00:00

How many people can fit: up to 20-30
