# Third-party image credits

**Status: empty. No third-party imagery ships on this site.**

Every photograph and every frame of video on domisignature.com is the client's
own work, served from `/public/media/`. The only non-photographic assets are the
brand's own marks in `/public/assets/img/` (`logo.png`, `mark.png`, and
`mark-bone.png`, which is derived from `mark.png`'s alpha channel).

This ledger exists so that the moment that stops being true, there is a required
place to record it — and so a reviewer can confirm at a glance that the site
makes no third-party claims.

---

## Why nothing was added

The Phase 6 imagery policy allows Tier 2 "atmosphere" imagery — texture, light,
landscape — from Unsplash or Pexels, provided it could never be mistaken for one
of our venues or a real client's celebration.

We had the option and declined it. The library holds 160+ genuine photographs
across three venues and seven events; every surface that could have taken an
atmosphere plate was better served by an actual frame from that venue. Importing
stock would have introduced a picture of somewhere that is not Crete, sitting on
a page that asserts facts about Crete — a small dishonesty for no gain.

## Rules, if this ever changes

1. **Source:** Unsplash or Pexels only. Self-host the file; never hotlink.
2. **Record it here before it ships** — one row per file, no exceptions:
   photographer, source URL, licence, and where it is used.
3. **Never adjacent to a venue fact.** Stock may not appear on a venue page, in
   a venue or event gallery, on a venue card, or anywhere a caption, capacity,
   coordinate or location sits beside it.
4. **Never a person.** No stock image may show a face, a couple, a ceremony, a
   table setting or anything that could read as one of our events or clients.
5. **No AI-generated photographic imagery**, from any source, ever. This is what
   removed `spire2.png` / `thspire2.png` from the site — see
   `design-review/imagery-report.md`.
6. Apply the same warm grade as the real photography, so a plate never announces
   itself as an import.
